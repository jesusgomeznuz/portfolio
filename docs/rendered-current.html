In 2024 I built a system to automate the parts of video production that weren't interesting — so I could spend time on the parts that were: the physics tuning, the music, the sequences that make a marble race satisfying to watch.

Music goes in. Simulation runs. Finished video comes out. No editing. The first one I published hit tens of thousands of views.
